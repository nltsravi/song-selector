Our Mission

Song Collection & Lyrics Library by Thalir Academy empowers devotional singers, congregations, and cultural custodians with instant access to curated hymns across classical and vernacular scripts: Tamil, Devanagari, and English transliteration.

Who We Are

Thalir Academy develops thoughtful, high-performance digital solutions that bridge cultural tradition and modern web applications. The Song Collection & Lyrics Library was created to make multi-script sacred verses universally searchable, customisable, and seamlessly exportable into beautifully formatted print and digital songbooks.

Key Capabilities

  • Multi-Script Fidelity: Instant script switching between Devanagari (हिन्दी / संस्कृत), Tamil (தமிழ்), and English transliteration, preserving lyrical phonetics and authentic poetic structure.
  • Curated Devotional Index: Categorised collections spanning Ganesha, Devi, Shiva, Guru, and general prayer chants with deity, raga, and tempo filters.
  • Dynamic Songbook Export: Customizable PDF and formatted Word Document generation with cover pages, dynamic tables of contents, running headers, and page numbering.
  • Google Drive Integration: Safe, automated archiving of exported songbooks directly into your authorized Google Drive workspace.
  • Offline-Ready & Responsive Design: Built from the ground up for desktop monitors, tablets, and smartphones, ready for future mobile packaging on iOS and Android.

Technology & Security

Built as a fast, secure static single-page application with zero third-party tracking, strict Google OAuth 2.0 identity verification, and client-side encryption. Your data stays in your control.
