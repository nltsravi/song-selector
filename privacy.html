1. Introduction

Thalir Academy ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Song Collection & Lyrics Library application (the "Service"). We are committed to transparency, data minimization, and protecting your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to authenticate you, authorize your access, and provide the core functions of the application:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in via Google OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect, we receive your email address, display name, and profile picture URL.
  • Application Activity: Custom song collections you create, reorder, or edit, which are stored locally in your browser's storage and synchronized with your account.
  • Google Drive Document Exports: When you generate PDF or Word exports, the document is attributed to your logged-in Google email ID and saved into Google Drive.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to any advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used for the following operational purposes:

  • Authentication & Access Control: To verify your identity and match your email address against the pre-authorized user list.
  • Personalized Library Experience: To associate your personal song collections with your email address.
  • Document Generation & Google Drive Storage: To store your exported song booklets in Google Drive under your authenticated account.
  • Service Diagnostics: To ensure reliable performance, mobile responsiveness, and troubleshoot any technical issues.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We employ modern security safeguards:

  • Local Browser Storage: Your song preferences, font size settings, and offline cached songs reside securely in your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB.
  • Transmission Security: All network communications use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • No Unnecessary Retention: We do not retain unnecessary personal data. You may sign out at any time to clear your active session credentials.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The Service does NOT use third-party advertising cookies, marketing tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts. Essential browser storage is utilized strictly to maintain your session state, language preferences, and offline caching via our Service Worker.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your data:

  • Sign Out & Session Removal: You can click "Sign Out" at any time from the header to remove your Google OAuth credentials from the browser.
  • Revoking Access: You can revoke this application's access at any time through your Google Account Security Settings.
  • Collection Deletion: You can delete any of your custom collections directly through the application interface.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
