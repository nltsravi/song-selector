## 3. Full Description
**Google Play Store & Apple App Store Full Description** *(Max 4,000 characters — ~2,150 characters)*:

```text
Song Collection & Lyrics Library is a complete multi-lingual devotional and classical songbook companion designed for singers, choir coordinators, satsang groups, and music enthusiasts. Effortlessly browse, search, organize, and export curated song collections in Tamil (தமிழ்), English, and Devanagari / Hindi (हिन्दी).

KEY FEATURES

• Multi-Lingual Lyrics Library
Read and switch seamlessly between Tamil, English transliteration, and Devanagari (Hindi) lyrics for every song in the library. Adjust lyrics font size on the fly for comfortable reading during live performances or group sessions.

• Smart Search & Tag Filtering
Quickly find songs by title, deity, raga, tempo, language availability, or custom tags. Switch between visual Card View and compact 2-Column List View to scan large repertoires with ease.

• Custom Song Collections
Create, edit, reorder, and manage personalized song collections for upcoming events, bhajans, concerts, or worship sessions. Add or remove songs in seconds and keep your collections organized.

• Professional PDF & DOCX Songbook Export
Generate print-ready PDF and Microsoft Word (DOCX) songbooks from any collection with a single tap:
  - Dedicated cover page with collection title and song index
  - Custom header emblem, subtle page watermark, and automatic page numbering
  - Native mobile Share Sheet integration to save, print, or share exported songbooks via WhatsApp, Email, or Google Drive

• Integrated Audio & Video Reference Links
Access reference audio tracks and video performances directly from song cards to practice melodies, pronunciation, and rhythm.

• Collaborative Lyrics & Title Editor
Authorized contributors can update multi-lingual titles, refine lyrics across Tamil, English, and Devanagari scripts, and manage song tags with automatic editor attribution.

• Offline-Ready & Cross-Platform
Built with offline caching so your song library and collections remain accessible anytime, anywhere—across Android, iOS, and Web.

Developed by Neolumina Technologies.
```
